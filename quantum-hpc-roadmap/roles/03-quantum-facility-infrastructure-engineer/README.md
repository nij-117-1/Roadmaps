# Role 3: Quantum Facility and Infrastructure Engineer

## What the role does

Plans, builds, and runs the physical home of a quantum computer: power, cooling water, cryogenic plant, floor and vibration, electromagnetic environment, and environmental monitoring. Works with vendors during site survey, installation, and daily operations.

The LRZ and IQM case study (arXiv:2509.12949) describes this work in detail. It is the role where your HPC facility experience (power, cooling, data-centre operations) transfers most directly. Most postings for this type of work sit under "quantum infrastructure", "quantum systems integration", or "HPC specialist".

## Physics depth required: Working on hardware, Conceptual on algorithms

You need to understand why the environment matters (temperature, magnetic fields, vibration) and how the cryostat and control electronics behave. You do not need algorithm depth.
