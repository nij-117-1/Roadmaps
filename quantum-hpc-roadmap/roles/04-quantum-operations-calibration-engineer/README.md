# Role 4: Quantum Operations and Calibration Engineer

## What the role does

Keeps quantum processors performing well day to day. Runs and automates calibration (frequencies, pulse amplitudes, readout), tracks qubit performance over time (T1, T2, gate and readout fidelity), detects drift, and schedules recalibration around user demand.

Postings include Q-CTRL's "Quantum Calibration Engineer" and roles at QPU vendors. This is a strong route for HPC operators because it is operations-heavy: monitoring, automation, scheduling, and incident handling.

## Physics depth required: Working

You need to understand what each calibration step measures and why its result changes. You do not need to derive algorithms, but you must read a Ramsey or Rabi experiment and know what it tells you.
