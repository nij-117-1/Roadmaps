# Role 4: Materials

## Free: qubit characterisation
- IBM Quantum Learning: *Use a Quantum Computer Today* (Olivia Lanes; hands-on experiments on real hardware)
- IBM Quantum Learning: *Quantum Computing in Practice* (noise, mapping, and error reduction)
- Qiskit documentation on noise models and error mitigation
- Krantz et al., "A Quantum Engineer's Guide to Superconducting Qubits" (free on arXiv), Sections on characterisation and gates

## Free: physics for calibration
- MIT OCW 8.04 (Zwiebach) and 8.05 for Rabi, Ramsey, and two-level dynamics (Phases 2 and 4 of the general roadmap)
- Susskind, *The Theoretical Minimum*, the Quantum Mechanics lectures on spin and precession

## Free: calibration and benchmarking papers
- Randomised benchmarking papers (search arXiv for "randomized benchmarking" and "interleaved randomized benchmarking")
- Papers on automated calibration of superconducting qubits (search arXiv for "automated calibration superconducting qubits")
- Papers on quantum volume and benchmarking (search arXiv)

## Free: data analysis and tooling
- SciPy documentation (curve fitting, optimisation)
- Grafana documentation (time-series dashboards)
- Slurm documentation (dependencies and scheduled jobs)

## Practical access
- IBM Quantum Open Plan (free; limited runtime but enough for hands-on characterisation practice)
- Vendor calibration documentation where available

## Paid / certification
- IBM Certified Quantum Computation using Qiskit v2.X Developer, Associate (useful; $200 USD per attempt; exam C1000-179)
- Vendor training for their control and calibration stacks, usually given to customers and partners

## Job market reading
- Q-CTRL and QPU vendor job postings for calibration and operations roles (search by "calibration engineer" and "quantum operations")
