# Role 5: Materials

## Free: pulse-level quantum programming
- Qiskit documentation (pulse module and current status; check whether pulse-level features are still supported in the version you use)
- IBM Quantum Learning: *Use a Quantum Computer Today* (hardware-level view)
- IBM Quantum Learning: *Quantum Computing in Practice* (mapping and hardware constraints)

## Free: pulse and control physics
- Krantz et al., "A Quantum Engineer's Guide to Superconducting Qubits" (free on arXiv), Sections on control and readout
- MIT OCW 8.06 (Harrow) lectures on time-dependent problems and Rabi oscillations
- Sakurai & Napolitano, Chapter 5 (time-dependent perturbation theory and two-level systems)

## Free: optimal control
- Khaneja et al., "Optimal control of coupled spin dynamics: design of NMR pulse sequences by gradient ascent algorithms" (2005; the GRAPE method, free on arXiv)
- Search arXiv for "optimal control superconducting qubits" for recent work

## Free: electronics and FPGA
- Free online introductions to digital design (search for "FPGA fundamentals" and "digital design basics")
- Vendor documentation for AWG and FPGA platforms (Qblox, Quantum Machines), where publicly available

## Free: real-time and systems
- Linux kernel real-time documentation (PREEMPT_RT)
- Your existing knowledge of low-latency HPC networking (InfiniBand and RDMA documentation)

## Paid / certification
- Vendor training courses for control stacks (usually customer-only)
- Electronics or FPGA courses at a local college or online (choose a course with lab work)

## Job market reading
- Quantum Machines and Qblox job postings for control software and FPGA roles
- Search for "quantum control software engineer" and "cryogenic control electronics" on quantumcareers.com
