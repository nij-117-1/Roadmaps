# Role 6: Materials

## Free: quantum error correction courses and tutorials
- IBM Quantum Learning: *Foundations of Quantum Error Correction* (John Watrous, free)
- CUDA-Q Academic: *Quantum Error Correction 101* (github.com/NVIDIA/cuda-q-academic, qec101 folder)
- CUDA-Q QEC documentation and the QEC 101 learning path (nvidia.github.io/cuda-quantum)
- Preskill's Caltech Ph219 notes, Chapter 7 (free PDF)

## Free: foundational theses and reviews
- Gottesman, *Stabilizer Codes and Quantum Error Correction* (PhD thesis, arXiv quant-ph/9705052)
- Fowler et al., "Surface codes: Towards practical large-scale quantum computation", Physical Review A, 2012 (free on arXiv)
- Dennis, Kitaev, Landahl, Preskill, "Topological quantum memory", Journal of Mathematical Physics, 2002 (free on arXiv)

## Free: decoders and real-time systems
- Search arXiv for "real-time decoding GPU quantum error correction" and "union-find decoder" for recent work
- Search arXiv for "minimum-weight perfect matching decoder" (PyMatching paper and documentation)
- NVIDIA quantum QEC blogs and GTC session recordings on real-time decoding

## Free: simulation tools
- Stim (Google, open source): documentation and tutorials at github.com/quantumlib/Stim
- PyMatching documentation (open source MWPM decoder)
- Qiskit documentation on noise models (for comparison)

## Textbooks
- Nielsen & Chuang, Chapter 10 (quantum error correction)
- Lidar & Brun (eds.), *Quantum Error Correction* (Cambridge, 2013), for depth and advanced topics

## Papers to read in order
1. Gottesman thesis (stabiliser formalism)
2. Dennis et al. (topological codes and threshold)
3. Fowler et al. (surface code practicalities)
4. Two recent papers on real-time GPU or FPGA decoders (search arXiv)

## Paid / certification
- IBM Certified Quantum Computation using Qiskit v2.X Developer, Associate (useful for the software side; $200 USD per attempt; exam C1000-179)
- No widely recognised quantum-specific QEC certification exists yet. Portfolio work is the main evidence for this role.
