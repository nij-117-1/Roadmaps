# Role 1: Quantum–HPC Systems Engineer

## What the role does

Builds, deploys, and operates hybrid platforms that combine GPU clusters with quantum processors and simulators. Owns the Linux, scheduler, networking, storage, and vendor integration for the quantum side of the facility.

This is the closest match to your current job. Some senior postings (for example at NVIDIA, D-Wave, and IonQ) describe it as a role that is "not a pure research role nor a traditional HPC admin role" and ask for extensive HPC experience, often 10 years or more. Target mid-level or lead roles first.

## Physics depth required: Working (deep for software and integration)

You need to understand what a quantum computer does and what it demands from infrastructure. You do not need to derive results.
