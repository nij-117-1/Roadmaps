# Role 1: Materials

## Free: quantum basics for operators
- IBM Quantum Learning: *Basics of Quantum Information and Computation*
- IBM Quantum Learning: *Use a Quantum Computer Today*
- IBM Quantum Learning: *Integrating Quantum and High-Performance Computing* (about 6 hours; most relevant)
- IBM Quantum Learning: *Quantum Computing in Practice* (mapping and noise)

## Free: simulators and platforms
- CUDA-Q documentation and applications: nvidia.github.io/cuda-quantum
- CUDA-Q Academic (GitHub: `NVIDIA/cuda-q-academic`), especially Quantum Algorithm Simulation 101
- Qiskit documentation and Runtime docs: docs.quantum.ibm.com
- NVIDIA quantum computing resources hub (blogs, GTC recordings, whitepapers)

## Free: quantum–HPC integration case studies
- arXiv:2509.12949, "First Practical Experiences Integrating Quantum Computers with HPC Resources" (LRZ and IQM). Read in full.

## Free: classical HPC tools you need to extend
- Slurm documentation: slurm.schedmd.com (GRES, partitions, accounting, job arrays)
- Apptainer documentation: apptainer.org
- Prometheus and Grafana documentation
